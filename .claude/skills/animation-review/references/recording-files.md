# What a recording holds

`scripts/record.mjs` writes one folder per recording (and one per trip or event with `all`). Every time in it is seconds after the mark, the moment just before the press; rows before the mark have negative times and show the place the recording leaves. Every position is in CSS pixels from the top left of the page, whatever `--dpr` is; `sheets.py` scales them to the frames.

| File               | Written by | What it is                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------ | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `meta.json`        | record.mjs | How it was recorded (mode, mission, stop or event, screen, steps, why it ended) and the build stamp (below).                                                                    |
| `events.json`      | record.mjs | The flight's events (id, title, mission second, story second, view, `keyMoment`, and `tourStop`: whether this flight's tour stops at it), the story clock's keys, its partings. |
| `timeline.tsv`     | record.mjs | The interface and the scene every 100 ms.                                                                                                                                       |
| `camera.tsv`       | record.mjs | The camera every frame the page runs (`requestAnimationFrame`), whether or not it draws.                                                                                        |
| `cast/pNNNNN.jpg`  | record.mjs | Every frame the page drew, in CSS pixels whatever `--dpr` is, named by milliseconds after the mark (`mNNNNN` before it).                                                        |
| `at-start.png`     | record.mjs | A screenshot just before recording (device pixels).                                                                                                                             |
| `end.png`          | record.mjs | A screenshot at the end.                                                                                                                                                        |
| `check.txt`        | check.py   | The report: build, frames, signals, the card against the clock, changes, pace, marks, a thinned camera table.                                                                   |
| `camera-table.tsv` | check.py   | `camera.tsv` turned into motion, one row a frame.                                                                                                                               |
| `marks.tsv`        | check.py   | The moments worth a closer look.                                                                                                                                                |
| `signals.json`     | check.py   | The signals, for the summary of a sweep.                                                                                                                                        |
| `sheet-NN.jpg`     | sheets.py  | The whole recording at 0.5 s, 12 tiles to a sheet, whole frames.                                                                                                                |
| `craft-NN.jpg`     | sheets.py  | The same times, each tile cropped around the followed craft (about eight times its size, so a 30 px craft is drawn about 80 px).                                                |
| `mark-NN-*.jpg`    | sheets.py  | Each marked moment at 0.1 s: whole frames for camera marks, cropped around the craft for craft marks.                                                                           |

Sheets are at most 1980 px a side, so the image reader shows them at full size. Each tile's label gives its time, the mission clock, the view, `ff` while fast forward shows, `stand-in` while the followed craft is drawn as a stand-in gliding round its ring (not at its true place, which races round the body), and `stopped` while the clock is still. A tile shows the frame on screen at its time, which is the last one drawn: a still page draws nothing, so a label that says `frame from 3.21 s` means nothing was drawn since then. While the clock runs that is a stall; while it is stopped it is rest.

## The build stamp

`meta.json` → `stamp`: the commit and branch of the checkout you ran from, how many files are changed (and the first twenty), when, the dev server's address, and `servedMatches`: whether the dev server serves this checkout. record.mjs compares a hash of a few files (`src/scene/stage.ts`, `src/app/tour.ts`, `src/app/player.ts`, `src/sim/story-time.ts`, `src/ui/interface.ts`) with what the server sends for them. When they differ it prints a warning: the recording shows some other tree, perhaps another agent's server, or yours half-saved. `load` and `cores` are the machine's load average and core count at the time; above one per core it warns, because other recorders slow every frame.

## timeline.tsv (every 100 ms)

| Column      | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `t`         | Seconds after the mark.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `clock`     | The mission time drawn, in seconds since liftoff.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `story`     | The story clock, in story seconds.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `playing`   | 1 while the clock runs (a tour trip, or Play).                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `speed`     | Story seconds per real second right now: the chosen rate, or a trip's pace (above 1 in a trip's hurry and in a run-up).                                                                                            |
| `pace`      | Mission seconds per real second right now (what fast forward is decided from).                                                                                                                                     |
| `view`      | `earth`, `moon`, `spacecraft` or `journey`.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `scale`     | `readable` or `true`.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `ff`        | 1 while the scene says fast forward shows (the yes/no the interface hears).                                                                                                                                        |
| `fastview`  | How far the ship view has moved to its steady fast-forward view, 0 to 1.                                                                                                                                           |
| `tween`     | 1 while a camera move is under way (a change of view, a re-aim, the return from the steady view).                                                                                                                  |
| `usermoved` | 1 once the visitor has moved the camera (drag, wheel, keys) since the last choice of view.                                                                                                                         |
| `followed`  | The craft the ship view follows now.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `counter`   | The tour's counter as shown ("Stop 5 of 12 On the way…"), or `-` when hidden.                                                                                                                                      |
| `button`    | The tour's main button (Next, Skip, Finish), or `-` when hidden.                                                                                                                                                   |
| `play`      | Play's label (Play or Pause), or `-` when hidden.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `card`      | The card's title, or `-` when the card is hidden (after the tour ends, say).                                                                                                                                       |
| `ending`    | 1 while the ending shows in the card's place.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `clear`     | The area the interface leaves uncovered: `x,y,WxH`, its top left corner and size. A change here moves the framing.                                                                                                 |
| `dock`      | The dock's top and height (`606+266`).                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `ships`     | Each craft: `id:cx,cy,WxH@fade` (its box's centre and size, and how opaque its model is drawn, 0 to 1), `id:-@fade` when not drawn, `id:gone` when it does not exist then. `+dot` when it shows as a small dot.    |
| `left`      | Parts left behind on the Moon: `part:cx,cy,WxH`, or `part:-` while not drawn.                                                                                                                                      |
| `labels`    | Their labels: `text@opacity`, separated by `; `.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `classes`   | The page's body classes (`is-ready` once the loading screen has gone, `is-touring`, ...).                                                                                                                          |
| `glide`     | Each craft: `id:laps` while its model is a stand-in gliding round its ring (the laps it has glided since it began; they never go down while it stands in), `id:-` while its model is at its true place, `id:gone`. |
| `laps`      | Each craft: `id:laps`, the laps its true place has flown round the body below it, as the scene counts them, whatever its model shows.                                                                              |
| `fflaps`    | The laps the followed craft has really flown in the fast forward now showing (`Stage.fastForwardLaps`; the interface shows the whole ones as "Fast forward · 12 orbits"), 0 when none shows.                       |

Only what is visible is read: a hidden button or card reads `-`, so the end of the tour does not look like its last stop.

The stand-in. While a craft is in fast forward its true place races round the body, so its model is drawn as a stand-in instead: it sets off from the true place at about the pace the craft was going, eases over a second to one steady pace of its own (one lap in `FAST_FORWARD_GLIDE_LAP_SECONDS` of real time), and when fast forward ends hands back to the true place with a dip: it fades out, moves while nothing is drawn, and fades back in (or eases across when it is within a few degrees). Under reduced motion there is none: the model is hidden and the ring stands alone. `ships` gives each box where the model is drawn, so the stand-in's place; `glide` says which boxes those are. A recording made before the stand-in has no `glide`, `laps` or `fflaps` column, and check.py reads it as having no stand-in.

## camera.tsv (every frame)

| Column             | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `t`                | Seconds after the mark, by the frame's own timestamp (the one the clock steps by), so a callback that runs late does not skew a rate.                                                                                                  |
| `clock`, `story`   | Mission and story time.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `playing`, `speed` | As in the timeline.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `view`, `followed` | As in the timeline.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `tween`            | 1 while a camera move is under way.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `usermoved`        | 1 once the visitor has moved the camera.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `fastview`         | The ship view's steady fast-forward view, 0 to 1.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `fade`             | How opaque the followed craft's model is drawn, 0 to 1; 0 while it is not drawn at all (before the stand-in, a hidden model kept the fade it last drew with).                                                                          |
| `ff`               | How far that craft is faded for fast forward, 0 to 1.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `laps`             | The laps its true place has flown around the body below it, as the scene counts them. Its rate is the true place's laps per second, which is on screen only while the model is drawn there (not while it is a stand-in).               |
| `near`             | `E` or `M`: the body nearer the followed craft (by height in that body's radii, as the scene decides).                                                                                                                                 |
| `ed ex ey er`      | Earth: the camera's distance from its centre in Earth radii, its centre on screen (`-` when behind the camera), its radius on screen.                                                                                                  |
| `md mx my mr`      | The same for the Moon, in Moon radii.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `sx sy`            | Where the followed craft's true place is on screen, or `-` when it does not exist or is behind the camera. In fast forward its model is drawn elsewhere (`dx dy`).                                                                     |
| `occluded`         | 1 when Earth or the Moon is between the camera and the followed craft's true place.                                                                                                                                                    |
| `tilt`, `tiltw`    | The craft's local up (away from the body below it) as the screen shows it, in degrees from straight up; `tiltw` is how much of it lies in the screen's plane (near 0 when the camera looks straight down, and the tilt means nothing). |
| `fx fy fz`         | The camera's view direction.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `ux uy uz`         | The camera's up direction.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `w h`              | The canvas's size.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `standin`          | 1 while the followed craft's model is a stand-in gliding round its ring, 0 while it is at its true place (`-` in a build without the stand-in).                                                                                        |
| `glide`            | The laps the stand-in has glided since it began (never going down while it stands in), `-` while there is none. Its rate is the model's laps per second on screen while it stands in.                                                  |
| `dx dy`            | Where the followed craft's model is drawn (`Stage.modelPlaces`, as last drawn): the stand-in's place in fast forward, beside its partner in a pair, else its true place; `-` when it does not exist or is behind the camera.           |
| `doccluded`        | 1 when Earth or the Moon is between the camera and where the model is drawn.                                                                                                                                                           |
| `dux duy duz`      | Which way from the centre of the body below it the model is drawn (a unit vector), so check.py can take the degrees it moves round the body between frames.                                                                            |
| `ring`             | While it stands in, how far the stand-in is from its ring as drawn, in pixels on screen: the nearest point of the ring in space, both projected. Its first second fades in from the true place, so it is off the ring then by design.  |

One case these do not cover: a craft with no stand-in of its own drawn along with its partner's (a pair, such as Eagle the moment it exists at undocking, riding with Columbia's stand-in) has `standin` 0. Its `dx dy` follow the partner's stand-in and it dips with the partner's hand-back, but its `lapsps` in camera-table.tsv is its true place's.

## camera-table.tsv (check.py)

One row a frame: `t`, `gap` (milliseconds since the frame before), `clock`, `story`, `playing`, `view`, `followed`, `tween`, `usermoved`, `fastview`, `fade`, and the columns below. Everything about where the craft is on screen (`sx sy`, `jump`, `where`, `arc`) is where its model is drawn, and `lapsps` is the pace of the model as drawn; the true place's are kept in `tx ty`, `twhere` and `truelapsps`. A recording made before the stand-in has only the true place, which was where its model was drawn.

| Column             | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `turn`             | How fast the camera's view direction turns, degrees a second (over at least 50 ms).                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `roll`             | How fast the camera rolls about its view direction, degrees a second.                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `tilt`             | The horizon's tilt in the ship view (see camera.tsv).                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `body`             | The body that matters: Earth in the Earth view, the Moon in the Moon view, otherwise the one nearer the followed craft.                                                                                                                                                |
| `dist`             | The camera's distance from that body's centre, in its radii.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `cover`            | The share of the screen covered by whichever body covers more (1 when the camera is inside one).                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `sx sy`            | Where the followed craft's model is drawn on screen (camera.tsv's `dx dy`).                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `jump`             | How far it moved on screen since the frame before, in pixels.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `where`            | `in` the clear area, `under` the interface (on the canvas but outside the clear area), `off` the canvas, `behind` the camera, `hidden` behind a body, `none`.                                                                                                          |
| `lapsps`           | Laps per second of the followed craft's model as drawn, over the last half second: its stand-in's glide while it is one (over the stand-in's time so far, once that is a quarter second), its true place's otherwise, never over a stretch that was partly a stand-in. |
| `standin`, `glide` | As in camera.tsv.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `arc`              | Degrees round the body below it that the model moved since the frame before.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `ring`             | Pixels the stand-in is off its ring (camera.tsv).                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `truelapsps`       | Laps per second of the true place, over the last half second, drawn or not.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `tx ty`, `twhere`  | The true place on screen and where it is, as `sx sy` and `where`.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## marks.tsv (check.py)

`kind`, `from`, `to`, `peak_t`, `peak`, `note`. The rules are rules of thumb that find moments to look at; a mark is a place to look and its absence is not a pass. The thresholds are at the top of `check.py`.

| Kind                               | Found when                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Standards |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| `jump`                             | The followed craft's model moves more than 40 px (or 4% of the diagonal) between two frames while drawn. The note says if the clock jumped, a frame was slow, or a stand-in began or handed back then.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | 18        |
| `turn`                             | The camera turns faster than 60°/s. The note says whether a camera move was under way.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | 11, 9     |
| `roll`                             | The camera rolls faster than 20°/s.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | 14        |
| `tilt`                             | In the ship view, settled and not fast-forwarding, the horizon is more than 25° off level.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | 14        |
| `swell`                            | A body covers more than 70% of the screen, or its share rises by 30 points within a second.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | 11        |
| `moving-at-rest`                   | The clock is stopped, the visitor has not moved the camera, and the camera still turns or a body slides on screen. The note says whether the tour was holding on its way or had arrived.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | 9, 10     |
| `re-aim`                           | A camera move starts in the Earth or Moon view with no change of view.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | 9         |
| `off`, `under`, `behind`, `hidden` | The drawn followed craft is off the canvas, under the interface, behind the camera, or behind a body, where its model is drawn: a stand-in in plain view whose true place is behind the Moon is not hidden.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | 16, 17    |
| `laps`                             | The drawn followed craft laps faster than standard 7 allows (once in 20 seconds; `LAP_LIMIT` in check.py), as drawn (`lapsps`): its stand-in's glide while it is one. In every view, the steady fast-forward view included.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | 7         |
| `pop`                              | A drawn craft's box changes size by half again in 100 ms. At a separation that can be the shape changing as a part falls away; the sheet tells.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | 18        |
| `vanish`                           | A drawn craft is not drawn 100 ms later, without fading and away from the edges.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | 18, 19    |
| `stall`                            | A page frame over 250 ms while the clock ran.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | all       |
| `glide-pace`                       | A stand-in's pace, taken over a second (or its time so far, once that is a quarter second), is faster than standard 7 allows; or, once its first second of easing in is over, it is more than 20% off its own usual pace (`GLIDE_STEADY`). A second that holds a frame over 0.12 s is not judged for steadiness: the scene moves the stand-in at most 0.1 s a frame, so it falls behind on a slow frame, and `stall` marks those. | 22        |
| `glide-stop`                       | While the clock runs, a stand-in goes back round its ring, or stands still for 0.25 s (`GLIDE_STOP_S`) over frames the page drew.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | 22        |
| `no-dip`                           | A model that is or was just a stand-in moves, between two frames both drawn at 0.5 or more, more than 3 degrees round the body plus what one lap in 20 s covers in that time (`NO_DIP_DEG`), or, with no camera move under way, more than 30 px for each 0.1 s between them (`NO_DIP_PX`). At a hand-back that is a move with no dip; as a stand-in begins, a start away from where the craft was.                                | 22        |
| `off-ring`                         | The followed craft's stand-in, drawn, is more than 6 px off its ring on screen (`OFF_RING_PX`) after its first second.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | 22        |

The `glide-*` and `no-dip` marks are looked for in the followed craft every frame (from camera.tsv, with degrees round the body) and in every other craft every 100 ms (from the timeline's `glide` and `ships`, in pixels only, judged more loosely since a sample shows the last frame drawn before it). `glide-pace` and `glide-stop` count only while the stand-in is drawn, as a model or as a dot.

Marks are not looked for while the loading screen still covers the scene (before `is-ready`). Under `--reduced-motion` every mark says that cuts are expected; there is no stand-in then, so none of standard 22's marks can fire.

## check.txt: the signals

The report opens with the build, the frames (stalls while the clock ran apart from rest while it was stopped, and slow page frames), and a list of signals: `FAIL` where a standard is plainly broken (the card names a moment the clock has not reached), `LOOK` where the marks found something to read the sheets for, `ok` where they found nothing, and `REDO` on the frames when fewer than five frames a second came while the clock ran: too few to judge motion by, and slow enough to change what the scene does. Each signal's name starts with the numbers of the standards in SKILL.md it bears on (`1 card`, `9-10 still`, `16-17 in frame`, ...). `22 glide` gathers the `glide-pace`, `glide-stop`, `no-dip` and `off-ring` marks; on a recording made before the stand-in it reads `-` (not recorded). Then the card against the clock stretch by stretch, every change in the timeline, the pace, the fast forwards, the marks, and the camera every quarter second and at every mark (`s` after the craft's place where it is a stand-in, and the true place's laps a second last).

The pace gives the fastest lapping of a drawn craft as drawn (a stand-in's glide while it is one) and, when there was a stand-in, the fastest lapping of a true place, drawn or not.

The fast forwards are listed one stretch a stand-in, for every craft: when it began and ended, the true laps that went by, and, for the followed craft, when the interface showed "Fast forward" and the orbits it counted to; how far the stand-in glided, its usual pace once eased in (within how much of it, over how many one-second windows, and how many were left out for a slow frame), and its fastest; and how it ended: its hand-back dipped (how long the model was below 0.5, how low, how far it moved unseen), eased across, or had no dip, or it was still a stand-in at the end, or a jump of the clock or the craft's going ended it. The figures come from every frame while the craft was followed, else from the timeline every 100 ms, as each line says. A hand-back measured on headless Chrome's slow frames lasts longer than `FAST_FORWARD_HANDBACK_SECONDS`, since the scene steps it at most 0.1 s a frame.

Given the folder of a sweep (`record.mjs ... all`), `check.py` checks every recording in it again and prints one line a recording with each signal's state; record.mjs prints the same at the end of the sweep. Run it after changing `check.py`, or to compare a sweep before and after a fix, without recording again.
